# Master Services Agreement

A sample agreement between Example Customer, Inc. ("Customer") and Example Vendor LLC ("Vendor") for the review example.

## 1. Services
1.1 Vendor provides the hosted software and support described in each order form.
1.2 Vendor may change the services at any time without notice.

## 2. Fees
2.1 Customer pays all fees within 15 days of each invoice. Late amounts accrue interest at 2% per month.
2.2 Vendor may raise fees on 10 days' notice.

## 3. Indemnity
3.1 Customer will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Vendor against any claim arising from Customer's use of the services, including claims caused by Vendor's negligence.
3.2 Vendor has no obligation to indemnify Customer.

## 4. Limitation of liability
4.1 Vendor's total liability under this agreement will not exceed the fees paid in the one month before the claim.
4.2 Customer's liability is unlimited.
4.3 Neither limit applies to Customer's payment obligations.

## 5. Term and termination
5.1 This agreement renews automatically for successive three-year terms.
5.2 Vendor may terminate for convenience on 5 days' notice. Customer may terminate only for Vendor's uncured material breach after 90 days' written notice.
5.3 On termination Vendor may delete Customer data immediately.

## 6. General
6.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
